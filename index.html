How I work

From a messy idea to a product people can use

  1. Discover

    Find the real problem before the brief hardens. At GIPC, "where can AI help?" turned into certificate forgery.

  2. Define

    Write requirements engineers can build and testers can check, with clear acceptance criteria.

  3. Prioritise

    Order work by what blocks launch, not by what's exciting. At HYRO, onboarding came before trading.

  4. Coordinate

    Keep founders, engineers, vendors and regulators on one plan, and chase blockers rather than wait.

  5. Validate

    Test every path end to end, triage bugs, and demo to stakeholders before anything ships.

  6. Launch

    Check readiness: docs, support, channels and a rollback plan, then release.

  7. Learn

    Bring feedback and usage back into the backlog, and change what needs changing.

About

From data to delivery to product

I studied mathematics at KNUST and started in data, building Power BI dashboards and loan-risk models, and automating reporting that saved an operations team about 12 hours a week.

That work pulled me toward the products behind the data. At Annologic I've led delivery across software, AI, Web3 and mobility projects, and managed a team of five. At Hyro I set up delivery for a regulated crypto exchange from the ground up. Alongside that, I'm a freelance product manager for Lepta QA and Lepta Studio, and I volunteer as product manager for Doctorevs and Dieti at Medirevs.

I'm PMP-certified, and most of my job is translation: turning a founder's goal into requirements engineers can build, and turning engineering trade-offs into decisions the business can make.
