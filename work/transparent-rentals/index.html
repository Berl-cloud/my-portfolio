Taking a chauffeur booking service in Accra from idea to 200 bookings

Transparent Rentals hires out cars with a professional driver in Accra at one flat, all-inclusive price. I led it from the first idea in October 2025 to a live service in January 2026, and through the changes that came after launch.

01The problem

Hiring a car with a driver in Accra usually starts with a negotiation. The price moves once fuel, waiting time or distance comes up, and visitors landing at the airport have the least information and the most at stake.

  • Price uncertainty. Quotes change once you're in the car, with fuel and extras added on top.
  • Reliability. No guarantee the driver or car turns up on time, especially for flights.
  • Friction. Back and forth by phone or chat before every trip.

Who it's for

  • Arriving visitors and diaspora needing airport pickups and drop-offs
  • Business travellers booking half-day and full-day hire in and outside Accra
  • Groups and families on multi-day trips
  • Leisure travellers heading to Cape Coast, Aburi or Akosombo

Full-day hire in Accra has turned out to be the largest segment by bookings.

02Evidence

Public complaints about hidden costs at existing rental firms showed us where the gap was: people didn't trust the price they were quoted. That set the product's one promise, an all-inclusive price with no surprises at the kerb.

What customers needed

  • Know the full price before booking
  • A driver who is there when the flight lands
  • Book fast, on the channel they already use

What we assumed

  • Customers value a price with nothing hidden
  • Airport transfers lead to repeat bookings
  • WhatsApp matters as much as the website

What launch has shown. Google reviewers describe the pricing as "fair and all-in-one". About 8 in 10 airport bookings are made before the customer lands. Many customers make first contact on WhatsApp and then book online, which confirmed that the two channels have to work as one service.

03Decisions and trade-offs

The promise is one flat, all-inclusive price. Most product decisions came from protecting that promise.

A flat $120 day, with a fuel allowance

DecisionDriver, fuel, WiFi and water are all included, with 100 km of fuel a day.

Trade-offDistance beyond 100 km is charged at $0.50/km, so the flat price stays sustainable on long days.

Accra-only core packages

DecisionAirport, half-day, full-day and multi-day packages stay inside Accra.

Trade-offTrips outside the city sit in their own tier from $150, so the Accra price stays simple.

Two vehicles, not a catalogue

DecisionA standard SUV at $120 a day and an executive model at $140.

Trade-offLess choice, but a faster decision and a simpler fleet to run.

Non-refundable customer cancellations

DecisionProtects a limited number of daily slots.

Trade-offA stricter policy, balanced by a full refund if we cancel.

04Execution

Booking had to fit in about a minute: service, then vehicle, then date and time, then confirm. Rules are built in, so Accra-only packages point you to the Outside Accra tier if you need to go further. Customers who prefer to chat can book on WhatsApp.

  1. Requirements

    I defined the flow, the packages and the rules the site had to enforce, such as Accra-only packages.

  2. Build and test

    The team built the site, booking flow, and driver and admin dashboards. I tested every package end to end, then made a real booking on the live site and paid through Paystack before we opened to customers.

  3. Launch

    We went live in late January 2026, and the first customer bookings came in that week.

  4. Iterate

    In week one I caught that a driver couldn't see the next day's booking on his dashboard, and we fixed it the same evening. In February we cut the booking flow from 3 screens to 2 to keep booking under 60 seconds.

What I did

What the team did

  • 3 developers and 1 designer built the site, booking flow and dashboards
  • The social media manager runs content and channels
  • The driver delivers the service in person

06What I learned

  • Transparent pricing is a product decision, not a marketing line. It shaped the packages, the fuel cap and the cancellation rules.
  • A simple offer is easier to build, sell and run. Six packages beat a custom quote for every trip.
  • Customers choose their channel. The web flow and WhatsApp have to work as one service.
